QOVRI

Engineered for What's Next

QOVRI is a premium digital engineering company — web, mobile, desktop, AI, and full-product design and development for entrepreneurs, startups, and enterprises worldwide.

The identity is built on one idea: an open ring, completed by a diagonal connector. It reads as a precise, geometric "Q" — a loop left intentionally unfinished, closed instead by a line of connection. Every other asset in this kit, from the favicon to the business card, extends that same shape vocabulary.
